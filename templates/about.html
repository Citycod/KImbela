Our Story

Kimbela was born from a simple observation: mature singles seeking authentic connections often find themselves navigating platforms that don't align with their values or life stage.

Founded in 2020, our platform emerged as a response to the growing need for a respectful, secure, and meaningful space where individuals 31+ could build genuine friendships based on shared values rather than superficial interactions.

What started as a vision to create a different kind of social platform has grown into a vibrant community of thousands who appreciate the depth, respect, and authenticity that Kimbela offers.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, respectful, and meaningful platform where mature singles can build authentic friendships based on shared values, mutual respect, and genuine connection.

Our Vision

To become the leading global community for mature singles seeking meaningful connections, setting new standards for authenticity and respect in social platforms.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped Kimbela into what it is today

2020

Kimbela was founded with a vision to create a different kind of social platform for mature singles. Launched with basic connection features and a strict 31+ age policy.

2021

Introduced community groups and interest-based discussions. Grew to 1000+ members with emphasis on safety features and content moderation.

2022

Launched mentorship program and premium services on personal growth and relationships.

2023

Reached 2000+ community members. Introduced advanced matching algorithms and enhanced privacy features. Received industry recognition for innovation.

2024

Expanded globally, celebrated thousands of success stories and meaningful connections.
