1. What is Kimbela?

Kimbela is a platform designed to help individuals aged 31 and above build meaningful friendships, connections, and communities. It may also allow users to discover products, services, and opportunities offered by other users through its marketplace features.

2. Who can join Kimbela?

Kimbela is intended for individuals who are at least 31 years old. By creating an account, users confirm that they meet this age requirement.

3. Is Kimbela a dating platform?

Kimbela is designed to help people form meaningful connections and friendships. While users may build deeper relationships through connections on the platform, Kimbela does not guarantee matches, relationships, or compatibility between users.

4. How do I create an account?

To create an account, users must provide basic registration information such as their name, email address, date of birth, and other profile details. Once registration is completed, users can build their profile and begin connecting with others.

5. Is my personal information safe?

Kimbela takes reasonable measures to protect user information and maintain platform security. Users are encouraged to review the Privacy Policy for details about how personal information is collected, used, and protected.

6. Can I choose what information appears on my profile?

Yes. While some information is required to create an account, users may choose what optional information to include in their profile. Users should exercise discretion when sharing personal details.

7. Can I delete my account?

Yes. Users may request deletion of their account at any time. Once an account is deleted, the profile will no longer be visible on the platform. Some information may be retained for legal, security, or administrative purposes.

8. Does Kimbela verify users?

Kimbela may introduce profile verification or identity verification features to improve trust and safety on the platform. Verification does not guarantee user behavior, intentions, authenticity, or outcomes. Users should exercise caution and personal judgment when interacting with others.

9. How can I report a user or suspicious activity?

If you encounter suspicious activity, harassment, fraud, or behavior that violates platform rules, you may report the user through the platform's reporting tools or contact support. Kimbela may review reports and take appropriate action, including warnings, removal of content, suspension, or banning of accounts.

10. Does Kimbela guarantee connections or matches?

No. Kimbela provides tools to help users discover and interact with others, but the platform does not guarantee successful connections, responses, friendships, or relationships.

11. What is the Kimbela marketplace?

The marketplace is a feature that allows users or businesses to offer products or services to other users on the platform. Sellers may promote their offerings through listings and other promotional features where available.

12. Does Kimbela sell products or services directly?

No. Products and services available on the marketplace are offered by independent sellers or service providers. Kimbela does not manufacture, supply, or directly provide these products or services.

13. How are payments handled on the marketplace?

Payments for marketplace products or services are typically arranged directly between the buyer and the seller unless otherwise specified on the platform. Kimbela may process payments for certain platform-related services such as subscriptions, promotions, or advertising features.

14. What happens if I have a dispute with a seller or another user?

Users are encouraged to first attempt to resolve disputes directly with the other party involved. If the issue cannot be resolved, users may report the matter to Kimbela for review. Kimbela may review reported activity and take appropriate action to protect the platform, but it does not act as a mediator or guarantor of transactions.

15. Are there paid features on Kimbela?

Kimbela may offer optional paid features such as promoted listings, sponsored posts, banner advertisements, or enhanced visibility for profiles or marketplace listings. These features are optional and may help increase visibility on the platform.

16. Does paying for promotion guarantee results?

No. Paying for promotional or visibility features does not guarantee responses, connections, sales, or engagement from other users.

17. What kind of content is not allowed on Kimbela?

Content that includes harassment, fraud, scams, hate speech, illegal activity, sexually explicit material, or abusive behavior is strictly prohibited. Users must comply with the Community Guidelines, Acceptable Use Policy, and Terms of Service.

18. Can Kimbela remove content or suspend accounts?

Yes. Kimbela reserves the right to remove content or suspend accounts that violate platform rules, threaten user safety, or harm the integrity of the platform.

19. How can I contact Kimbela?

If you have questions, need assistance, or wish to report an issue, contact support at support@kimbela.com. Kimbela is operated by Tutaf International Limited under the brand name Kimbela Hub.
