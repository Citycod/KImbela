1. Community Commitment

Kimbela is built on respect, trust, and meaningful human connection. Every user plays a role in maintaining a safe, respectful, and welcoming environment.

2. Respectful Conduct

  • Communicate respectfully and kindly
  • Respect differences in beliefs, backgrounds, and opinions
  • Engage in constructive and appropriate conversations

3. Honesty and Authenticity

  • No fake profiles or impersonation
  • No false or misleading personal information

4. Harassment and Abuse

Harassment, bullying, threats, repeated unwanted messages, or personal attacks are strictly prohibited and may result in suspension or removal.

5. Prohibited Content

  • Nudity, pornography, sexual solicitation
  • Hate speech or discrimination
  • Illegal activities, scams, fraudulent schemes
  • Harmful or deceptive content

6. Marketplace and Financial Conduct

  • No financial scams or deceptive requests
  • No misleading listings or fraudulent offers
  • No pressure tactics to force purchases or payments

7. Advertising and Promotions

Paid promotions must be honest and transparent. Misleading advertisements are prohibited.

8. Political Misuse

  • No misinformation or political manipulation
  • No coordinated disruption of political processes

9. User Safety

Users are responsible for their own safety. Avoid sharing sensitive information and report suspicious behavior.

10. Reporting Violations

Users may report violations. Kimbela may review, remove content, suspend accounts, or permanently ban users.

11. Moderation and Enforcement

Kimbela may take enforcement actions including warnings, content removal, suspension, or termination without prior notice when necessary.

12. Appeals

Users may request a review of enforcement decisions by contacting support. Kimbela retains final authority.

13. Updates

Kimbela may update these guidelines at any time. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

14. Community Vision

Kimbela is built to foster genuine human connection. Users are encouraged to review Safety Tips for guidance on interacting safely on and off the platform.
