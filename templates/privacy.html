Overview

Kimbela is a digital platform owned by Tutaf International Limited and operated under the brand name Kimbela Hub. References to “Kimbela,” “Kimbela Hub,” “we,” “our,” or “us” refer collectively to the Kimbela platform and its operating brand managed by Tutaf International Limited.

By creating an account or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Personal Information

  • First name, last name, email address
  • Date of birth, gender, marital status
  • Educational level, occupation

Optional Profile Information

  • Religion (optional), ethnicity (optional)
  • Interests and hobbies
  • Profile photo or profile description

Location Information

  • Country, state or province, city

Account Security Information

  • Password or login credentials
  • Login activity and account activity within the platform

Payment and Transaction Information

  • Marketplace subscriptions
  • Sponsored posts and banner advertisements
  • Promoted listings and paid matchmaking features

Payment processing may be handled by trusted third-party payment providers.

Technical Information

  • IP address, browser type, device type
  • Pages visited, date and time of visits

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable users to build profiles and connect with others
  • Facilitate interactions between users
  • Improve functionality and performance
  • Prevent fraud and maintain safety
  • Provide support and respond to inquiries
  • Process payments and manage paid services
  • Support matchmaking and discovery features
  • Display relevant sponsored content and promotions

3. Profile Visibility

Kimbela is a connection platform. Information included in a user’s profile may be visible to other users as part of discovery features. Users should exercise discretion when choosing what information to include.

4. Sensitive Personal Information

Some profile information (e.g., religion or ethnicity) may be sensitive. Providing this information is optional. Kimbela does not sell or distribute sensitive personal information to third parties.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

Trusted providers (hosting, security, payment processing, analytics, advertising) may access information necessary to perform their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect the rights and safety of Kimbela and its users.

6. Data Security

Kimbela takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information, but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze user interactions
  • Enhance user experience and promotional features

Users may disable cookies in their browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

8. Age Requirement

Kimbela is intended for individuals aged 31 or older. Accounts found to belong to users under 31 may be removed.

9. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion at any time. Profile information will be removed from public visibility, but some information may be retained for legal, security, or administrative purposes.

10. Third-Party Links

Kimbela may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised date.
