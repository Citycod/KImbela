1. Take Time to Build Trust

Get to know people gradually. Be cautious if someone pressures you to move conversations off-platform quickly.

2. Protect Your Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal details (home address, financial information, IDs) too early.

3. Watch for Red Flags

  • Requests for money or financial help
  • Inconsistent information
  • Pressure to move off-platform
  • Requests for sensitive information

4. Be Careful with Financial Requests

Do not send money to people you met online. Report suspicious requests immediately.

5. Meet in Public Places

  • Choose public locations
  • Avoid isolated places
  • Arrange your own transportation
  • Tell a friend or family member your plans

6. Trust Your Instincts

If you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, end the conversation, leave the situation, and report the user.

7. Report Suspicious Behavior

Reporting helps maintain a safe environment. Use the platform’s reporting tools or contact support.

8. Your Safety Comes First

Kimbela does not verify all users. All interactions occur at your own risk. Always prioritize your safety.
