1. Purpose of the Platform

Kimbela is designed to help individuals aged 31+ build meaningful friendships, connections, and communities, and may also facilitate marketplace interactions.

By accessing or using Kimbela, you agree to comply with this policy, as well as the Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and other applicable policies.

2. Prohibited Activities

Illegal Activities

  • Promoting or engaging in illegal activities
  • Selling illegal goods or services

Fraud and Scams

  • Defrauding or deceiving other users
  • Financial scams or fraudulent schemes

Harassment and Abuse

  • Bullying, intimidation, threats
  • Repeated unwanted messages

Sexually Explicit Content

  • Pornography or sexual solicitation
  • Sexually explicit images, videos, or messages

This restriction does not apply to legitimate licensed counseling or therapy services that comply with applicable laws.

Hate Speech and Discrimination

Promoting hatred or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, nationality, disability, or other personal characteristics is prohibited.

Misrepresentation

  • Providing false personal information
  • Creating fake profiles or impersonating others

Platform Manipulation

  • Manipulating matchmaking or connection systems
  • Creating multiple accounts for deceptive purposes
  • Using bots or automated scripts

Spam and Unsolicited Promotion

  • Sending spam or mass marketing without consent
  • Posting misleading promotional content

Unauthorized Data Collection

Collecting or harvesting personal data without permission is prohibited.

Security Violations

  • Unauthorized access attempts
  • Malicious software, viruses, or harmful code

3. Marketplace Conduct

Marketplace sellers must not misrepresent offerings, sell illegal goods, or engage in deceptive practices. Transactions are between buyers and sellers; Kimbela does not control or guarantee outcomes.

4. Advertising and Promotional Activities

Ads and promotions must be truthful and comply with platform policies. Kimbela may review, approve, reject, or remove content at its discretion.

5. Reporting Violations

Users may report violations. Kimbela may review and take action as needed to protect users and the platform.

6. Enforcement

Enforcement actions may include warnings, content removal, temporary suspension, permanent bans, or feature restrictions.

7. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted with a revised date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
